# 📸 AURA CINEMATICS — Luxury Wedding Photography & Films Platform

A modern, responsive, zero-cost web platform crafted for professional wedding photographers and cinematographers. Built with luxury dark aesthetics, warm gold accents, and a comprehensive suite of client & studio tools.

---

## 🌟 Key Features Built-In

### 1. 🖼️ Client-Facing Showcase
- **Hero Showcase:** Royal typography, live availability countdown, and direct call-to-actions.
- **Filterable Portfolio:** Filter by *Candid Emotions, Pre-Wedding, 4K Cinematic Films, Haldi & Rituals, Reception*.
- **4K Cinema Player:** In-browser modal video streaming for wedding trailers & teasers.
- **High-Res Lightbox:** View and download high-resolution master stills with one click.
- **Storyteller Section:** Photographer bio, cinema camera gear breakdown (Sony Cinema Line, DJI Mavic 3 Cine, Profoto lighting), and verified awards.

### 2. 💳 Transparent Packages & Price Breakdown
- **Timeless Photography:** Pure candid stills, coverage hours, deliverables, raw SSD files.
- **Cinematic Filmography:** 4K video teaser, full documentary film, licensed drone coverage.
- **Royal Combo Deluxe (Most Popular):** 5-crew team, 40-sheet Canvera leather album, drone flythroughs, priority delivery.
- **Palace Destination Elite:** Multi-day coverage, same-day edit teaser (SDE), VIP cloud portal.

### 3. 📅 Appointment Booking & Location System
- **Interactive Booking Wizard:** Select wedding date, venue, city, package tier, and custom requests.
- **Advance Token Calculation:** Calculates 25% advance deposit to lock the date on the calendar.
- **Auto WhatsApp Sync:** Generates a pre-filled WhatsApp message sent directly to the photographer.

### 4. 💰 Online Payment & 0% Fee UPI QR
- **Direct Bank UPI QR Code:** Generates dynamic UPI payment QR codes (`upi://pay`) for Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and BHIM.
- **Zero Commission:** 100% of client money transfers directly to the photographer's bank account without gateway fees.
- **Transaction Receipt & Confetti:** Celebratory confirmation modal with downloadable booking receipt.

### 5. 🔲 Unique Client QR Codes & Digital Photo Delivery
- **Unique QR per Wedding:** Every client gets a dedicated QR code (e.g. `?gallery=kabir-mehr-2026`).
- **In-Browser QR Scanner:** Visitors can scan QR cards with their device camera or type their 6-digit access code (e.g. `KM2026`).
- **Private Gallery View:** Protected with an optional 4-digit PIN.
- **Instant Photo Downloads:** High-res master ZIP download + social media format.
- **Printable Table Tent Cards:** Photographer can print high-resolution QR table cards for wedding guest tables so guests can scan and download photos right during the reception!

### 6. 📊 Private Photographer Admin Dashboard & Analytics Hub
- **Access via Secret Button or Login Modal:** Pre-filled with demo credentials:
  - **Email:** `admin@auracinematics.com`
  - **Password:** `admin123`
- **Live Business Analytics (Chart.js):**
  - **Financial Metrics:** Total Booked Revenue, Advance Collected in Bank, Pending Balances.
  - **Shoot Counters:** Total completed weddings, upcoming wedding countdown.
  - **Engagement Tracker:** Total QR code scans, guest photo downloads.
  - **Interactive Charts:** Monthly revenue trend line chart & package popularity doughnut chart.
- **Management Tabs:**
  - **Bookings Tab:** View all client bookings, accept/reject, call client, or chat on WhatsApp.
  - **QR Code Generator:** Create new client galleries, link Google Drive folders, and print table cards.
  - **Portfolio Manager:** Add new photos or YouTube cinematic trailers; delete items.
  - **Pricing Editor:** Change package prices and advance deposit amounts in real time.
  - **Studio & UPI Settings:** Update photographer name, phone, WhatsApp number, and direct UPI ID.
